### Hey, I'm Sahil 👋

I'm a Health Data Science MS student at Saint Louis University, focused on turning real hospital and public health data into models that hold up to scrutiny. I work across Python, R, SQL, and BigQuery, and I publish my projects with the paper, the code, and the caveats all in one place.

I care about three things:

- **Honest evaluation** — cross-validation matters more than a clean test split, and small-sample limitations get said out loud.
- **Reproducibility** — every model I publish has the script that produced it.
- **Real data** — I work with public health datasets (HCAI, CDC SAMMEC, CMS Hospital Compare), not toy CSVs.
